White-label CRM platform: localization, site generator, demo seeding, Telegram

The same agency resells a white-label HighLevel under its own brand to Ukrainian businesses. Their clients needed the interface in their language, sites they can edit themselves, demo accounts for sales calls, Telegram as a real channel, and clean imports of their existing data. Ongoing work since September 2026.

  • RoleSole engineer; the agency owner sets priorities, I design and ship
  • ConstraintNo hacks clients can see: native features, custom values, or one line of agency JS
  • Scale35,875 UI strings · 104 site sections · 2,366 imported companies
  • StackHighLevel API v2, custom JS, Workers, D1, Durable Objects, Python, Gemini

1. Interface localization, 35,875 strings

The platform has no Ukrainian locale. I catalogued every native UI string across 85 modules (35,875 of them), translated the catalogue with an LLM against a glossary of the agency's terms, and shipped the dictionary as a single line of agency-level custom JS with a UK | EN toggle.

  • Matching is exact-string and template-based (34,169 exact entries, 2,130 templates), so user data is never touched: a contact named "Dashboard" stays "Dashboard".
  • An admin page lets the agency override any term without a developer.
  • 34,868 strings translated (97%); 95% of screens covered across 151 pages and modals; live for all agency users since 14 October 2026.

2. Site generator on native builder sections

Clients wanted sites they can edit without a developer. Instead of custom code, I built a kit of 104 sections in 5 styles on the platform's own builder, driven by 10 shared color roles and custom values. A short brief goes through Gemini and comes back as a page assembled from those sections; the client edits it in the builder like any other page.

3. Demo accounts in 15 minutes

Sales calls need a sub-account that looks alive. One seeding engine creates companies, contacts, two pipelines with deals, calendars, invoices and conversations for a chosen niche (construction and IT services so far) in about 15 minutes, and can wipe everything it created by run label.

4. Telegram inside the CRM

Telegram became a channel in the conversations inbox through a custom conversation provider on a Durable Object, and a deal moving to a new stage sends the contact a Telegram message with buttons. Details are in the recruiting bot case, which runs on the same bridge.

5. Data in: imports and lead lists

  • Spreadsheet import: any xlsx/csv into companies, contacts and deals; resumable, deduplicated, with cleanup by run label. 81 tests that run without network; full dry run on a lab account before touching a client.
  • Public-procurement lead list: 4,176 companies parsed from the national procurement portal, 2,366 imported after cleaning, 321 of them IT companies for a targeted campaign.
  • "Fill from registry" button on the company card: looks up the national company registry (2 million records sharded into 15,000 pieces) and fills the legal fields.

Result

  • Localization is live for every agency user; new clients get the platform in their language on day one.
  • Every piece has a rollback: the dictionary is one JS file, imports are reversible by run label, seeds can be wiped.
