Driver and dispatcher apps for a US trucking dispatch SaaS

A US logistics company runs a dispatch product for carriers. Drivers and dispatchers needed to run loads from their phones: statuses, proof-of-delivery documents, receipts, pay and chat, often with a weak signal on the road. I joined the product team in October 2026.

  • RoleLead author of the driver app in a team of eight contributors; features in the dispatcher app; one backend connector
  • My commitsDriver app 242 of 278 · dispatcher app 38 · backend 42 (Oct–Nov 2026)
  • Quality gate997 unit tests (driver), ~390 (dispatcher), Maestro e2e on an emulator, review, GitLab CI
  • StackExpo SDK 57, RN 0.86, React 19, TypeScript, node:test, Maestro, Python backend

Driver app

  • Loads and stops with "I'm here" / "Leaving" saved offline and sent when the signal returns, in order, without duplicates.
  • Document scanner: four-corner crop, up to 10 pages in one PDF, receiver signature, proof-of-delivery upload with progress; receipts for lumper, scale and fuel.
  • Pre-trip and post-trip inspection with the federal checklist, weekly pay, chat with dispatch, past loads, reference numbers per stop, expiring-document cards.
  • Biometric login, light and dark theme, Spanish localization.

Dispatcher app

On the dispatcher side I shipped fuel-advance approvals with a confirmation step, rate-confirmation upload from the phone, truck maintenance flags and board features. The dispatcher app's main author is a teammate; my part is 38 commits.

Backend

A connector from the Python backend to an ELD telematics provider: per-key rate limits, retries on 429 and 5xx, deduplication of positions, heading calculated from the track. Merged to main with the backend suite green at 1,560 tests.

How changes ship

Every merge request goes through a TypeScript check, unit tests, Maestro end-to-end flows on an Android emulator against a mock proxy (no calls to real environments), and code review. Over 60 merge requests merged by late October; 997 unit tests in the driver app by November.
