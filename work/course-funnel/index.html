Course sales funnel: site, bank payments, access delivered by a Telegram bot

A fitness trainer in Kyiv sells an online course (Pilates, stretching, functional). She wanted a simple funnel: a page, a payment, and the course opens in Telegram where her clients already are. No accounts, no passwords, nothing for her to administer by hand.

The flow

  • Site: landing with program, trainer, testimonials and three tiers; legal pages; Meta Pixel. The checkout route collects name, email and phone and redirects to the bank's payment page.
  • Payment: Monobank invoice; the webhook verifies the bank's signature before anything happens. The owner gets a Telegram notification when a payment starts and when it succeeds.
  • Access: the site asks the bot's API for a one-time token (idempotent on payment id, so a repeated webhook can't issue two) and shows a deep link. Opening it in Telegram binds the course to whoever opened it; a /activate command is the fallback for a lost link. Access expires per tier (30 or 90 days).
  • Bot: catalogue of groups and videos gated by tier, admin menu inside Telegram for the owner (upload, group, grant access with the native user picker), payment audit log, rate limits in Redis.

Taking over the server

In September 2026 the second developer handed the bot over. I moved it to a new VPS with the same bot token (Telegram file ids are bound to it), a full database dump and a single poller, so nothing was lost: 109 videos and every active access came across. The stack runs in Docker behind Caddy with automatic TLS; migrations are pinned so a rebuild can't take the bot down.

Honest notes

  • The bot's code base is mostly the second developer's; my part is the site, the payment-to-access handoff, the admin picker and operations.
  • Sales volume is the client's business and is not published here.
