Gated recruiting community: a Telegram bot that lives in the CRM

An agency launched a closed Telegram community of vetted salespeople. They needed an application form at the door, manual approval, job posts from hiring companies, and every person and application in the CRM without duplicates, so the sales team works from the CRM and never from the chat.

  • RoleSolo: bot, CRM model, workflows, tests, deploy on the agency's account
  • Quality192 automated tests on mocked Telegram, CRM and DB; 14 bugs found before release
  • StorageMoved from KV to D1 after KV lost form steps when users answered fast
  • StackTelegram Bot API, HighLevel API v2, workflows → webhooks, Workers, D1, node:test

What I built

  • Door: a join request opens a short application in the bot (candidate or hiring manager). The answers become a contact and an opportunity in the CRM, in a dedicated field folder instead of tags.
  • Moderation with inline buttons for admins; approved members get an invite link.
  • Job posts: a wizard that publishes a vacancy into the group with "Apply" and "Refer someone" buttons. Applications go to the vacancy author in Telegram and by email through the CRM's conversations API. Vacancies and Referrals are custom objects linked to contacts and deals.
  • No duplicates: CRM upsert matches only email and phone, so the bot keeps its own deduplication index.
  • Status back to the candidate: a CRM workflow on pipeline stage change calls the bot's webhook, and the candidate gets a status update in Telegram.

Testing that found real bugs

192 tests run against mocked Telegram, CRM and database, no network. Before release they caught 14 bugs: races in moderation and publishing, missing timeouts on CRM calls, messages over Telegram's length limit, lost form state in KV under fast replies. All fixed; the last one is why storage moved to D1.

Result

  • In production on the agency's own Cloudflare account; the sales team recruits from the CRM pipeline, not from the chat.
