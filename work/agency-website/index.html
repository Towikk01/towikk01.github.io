Agency website: 29 pages, live motion, and an AI chat that books meetings

A Ukrainian agency that resells a white-label CRM platform had its site on WordPress and Elementor. They wanted a site on code: fast, with real motion, a pricing matrix clients can read, and leads that land in their own CRM instead of an inbox.

Open the live site
  • RoleSolo: design-to-code, motion, content migration, lead backend, deploy
  • Timeline24 Oct – 9 Nov 2026, 164 commits; live 30 Oct
  • Scale29 pages, pricing matrix of 39 rows, ~265 KB gzip of JS
  • StackNext.js 16, React 19, TS, Tailwind 4, GSAP 3, Lenis, Workers, D1, Gemini

Problem

The old site was a WordPress theme with Elementor blocks: slow, hard to change, and every lead ended up as an email. The agency sells a CRM, so a site that didn't use their own CRM for leads looked wrong to prospects.

What I built

  • 29 pages on Next.js 16 App Router: home, platform, industries, integrations, pricing, docs, news, contact and legal pages. Content was migrated from the source site, not rewritten; the site is in Ukrainian like the original.
  • Motion that respects the device: a particle preloader on Canvas 2D (no three.js), scroll-pinned scenes on GSAP ScrollTrigger, a liquid-glass UI layer, smooth scroll on Lenis. prefers-reduced-motion turns all of it into a short fade and static states.
  • Lead pipeline on a Cloudflare Worker: a form submission becomes a deal and a tag in the agency's CRM and a Telegram notification to the team.
  • AI assistant for visitors: a site chat on Gemini that answers from a knowledge base in D1 and can book a meeting into the team calendar. Guard rails: 1,500 questions a day per site and 150 per IP; bookings limited to 2 a day per IP and 15 per site; a cron every two minutes reconciles bookings.
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Worker with static assets, custom domain on root and www, preview environment for review before each release.
  • Hardening after launch: iOS Safari rendering fixes and performance passes in the first two weeks.

Result

  • Live at urbrave.com since 30 October 2026, opened page by page over two days so the agency could review each one.
  • All JS chunks ≈ 265 KB gzip; the home page prerenders statically.
  • The chat module ships with 19 automated tests; lead and booking modules have their own suites.
The agency's brand is public, so the site is shown as is. The CRM behind it and its data are not part of this page.
